# Lit LCD + lamp colours (AP1)

Warm amber-on-black, not neon. Unlit ticks are dim ghosts. Lit segments get a
soft orange bloom behind the bar.

## Lamp colours

| Colour | Lamps |
| --- | --- |
| Red | BRAKE, battery, oil, door, seatbelt, SRS |
| Amber | ABS, CEL, MAINT, EPS |
| Green | immobilizer key, turn arrows |
| Blue | high beam |

Icons are drawn as compact pictograms in the bezel strip (not labelled chips
on the LCD). Vector-style PNG extracts live in `assets/icons/`.

Fuel and TEMP stay **straight horizontal** bars on the LCD baseline.
